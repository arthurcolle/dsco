## 1. Executive summary

`[Customer]` currently performs `[named workflow]` through `[brief current process]`. The process requires `[repositories, systems, evidence sources]` and creates `[time, reliability, risk, or throughput cost]`.

Distributed Systems, Inc. proposes a 30-day DSCO Governed Workflow Pilot. The pilot will configure and evaluate one bounded AI-assisted workflow inside an agreed customer-controlled environment. The objective is to determine—using agreed cases and reviewable evidence—whether DSCO can improve `[target outcome]` without exceeding the documented authority boundary.

This proposal is a fixed-scope deployment and evaluation engagement. It does not promise a generalized autonomous system, unrestricted production access, security certification, or output correctness without customer review.

## 3. Deliverables

1. **Deployment and data-flow design** for the agreed environment.
2. **DSCO configuration** with minimum necessary project, tool, and evidence access.
3. **Documented authority profile** including capability limits and human approval points.
4. **One bounded workflow** for the scope above.
5. **Evaluation plan and results** against agreed cases.
6. **Operator runbook** covering startup, normal use, review, known limitations, and stop/rollback procedures.
7. **Final evidence report** with results, artifacts, failures/limitations, and recommendation.
8. **Final handoff session** for designated customer operators.
